## 3. Meeting Discussion

The team discussed the Afghan Antiques Store Management System, which will allow customers to browse antique products and place orders, while store staff/admins manage products and orders.

The project was divided into the following three sprints:

### Sprint 1: Foundation and Customer Accounts

**Focus:**
- Set up the project structure and database.
- Prepare the homepage and product catalog foundation.
- Implement customer registration.
- Implement customer login and logout.

**Expected outcome:** A basic system foundation with a working customer account process.

### Sprint 2: Product Discovery and Store Features

**Focus:**
- Allow customers to browse products.
- Display product photos, descriptions, and prices.
- Add product search.
- Work on shopping cart and related store features, according to priority and available time.

**Expected outcome:** Customers can explore products and use the planned shopping features.

### Sprint 3: Cart, Orders, Administration, and Finalization

**Focus:**
- Complete shopping cart and order-related functionality.
- Implement selected administrator features.
- Integrate frontend and backend components.
- Conduct testing and fix identified bugs.
- Complete project documentation and prepare the final demonstration/submission.

**Expected outcome:** An integrated and tested project with documentation and a final demonstration prepared.

## 4. Decisions Made

- The project would be organized into three sprints.
- Each sprint would have a defined focus and expected outcome.
- The team would coordinate frontend and backend development.
- The team would integrate and test components during development and before final submission.
- Project documentation and demonstration preparation would be included in the final sprint.
